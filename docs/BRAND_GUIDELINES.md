# CoolTunnel brand guidelines

## Core identity

- Product: **CoolTunnel**
- Endorsed lockup: **CoolTunnel by Cool Plugins**
- Tagline: **Share local sites instantly**
- Publisher: **Cool Plugins LLP**
- Product URL: <https://coolplugins.net/cooltunnel/>

Use `CoolTunnel` as one word with capital C and T. Do not abbreviate it in
public copy.

## Visual language

- Deep navy: `#073F78`
- Brand blue: `#0870D2`
- Sky blue: `#32A7FF`
- Tunnel green: `#52E4B2`
- Ink: `#10233F`
- Cloud white: `#F8FBFF`

The icon combines a cloud, a routed tunnel path and a connection point. Keep
clear space equal to one quarter of the icon width around the mark.

## Logo files

- `cooltunnel-icon.svg` — application and favicon mark
- `cooltunnel-logo-horizontal.svg` — primary product lockup
- `cooltunnel-logo-stacked.svg` — centered and square placements
- `cooltunnel-logo-monochrome.svg` — one-color printing
- `cooltunnel-social-preview.svg` — 1200×630 release and sharing artwork

Do not recolor individual pieces, stretch the mark, add shadows outside the
approved application icon, or place the full-color mark on low-contrast blue.
